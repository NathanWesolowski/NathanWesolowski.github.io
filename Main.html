SCP Object Database

SCP-002 EUCLID

The "Living" Room

SCP-002 resembles a tumorous, fleshy growth with a volume of roughly 60 m³. An iron valve hatch on one side leads to its interior, which appears to be a standard low-rent apartment.

SCP-049 EUCLID

Plague Doctor

SCP-049 is a humanoid entity, roughly 1.9 meters in height, which bears the appearance of a medieval plague doctor. While SCP-049 appears to be wearing the thick robes and the ceramic mask indicative of that profession, the garments instead seem to have grown out of SCP-049's body over time.

SCP-073 EUCLID

"Cain"

SCP-073 appears to be a heavily-tanned male of Arabic or Middle Eastern descent in his early thirties. His hair and eyes are a copper color. He is otherwise totally unremarkable in appearance.

SCP-087 EUCLID

The Stairwell

SCP-087 is an unlit platform staircase. Stairs descend on a 38 degree angle for 13 steps before reaching a semicircular platform approximately 3 meters in diameter. Descent direction rotates 180 degrees at each platform.

SCP-096 EUCLID

The "Shy Guy"

SCP-096 is a humanoid creature measuring approximately 2.38 meters in height. Subject shows very little muscle mass, with preliminary analysis of body mass suggesting mild malnutrition. Arms are grossly out of proportion with the rest of the subject's body.

SCP-106 KETER

The Old Man

SCP-106 appears to be an elderly humanoid, with a general appearance of advanced decomposition. This appearance may vary, but the "rotting" quality is observed in all forms. SCP-106 causes a "corrosion" effect on all solid matter it touches, engaging a physical breakdown in materials.

SCP-173 EUCLID

The Sculpture

Moved to Site-19. Origin is as of yet unknown. It is constructed from concrete and rebar with traces of Krylon brand spray paint. SCP-173 is animate and extremely hostile. The object cannot move while within a direct line of sight.

SCP-682 KETER

Hard-to-Destroy Reptile

SCP-682 is a large, vaguely reptile-like creature of unknown origin. It appears to be extremely intelligent and was observed to engage in complex communication. SCP-682 has a hatred of all life, which has been expressed in several interviews.

SCP-999 SAFE

The Tickle Monster

SCP-999 appears to be a large, amorphous, gelatinous mass of translucent orange slime. Subject's temperament is best described as playful and dog-like: when approached, SCP-999 will often react with overwhelming elation.

Foundation Mission Statement

The SCP Foundation exists to contain anomalous objects, entities, and phenomena that threaten the normalcy of human existence. We operate in secret to preserve the veil of reality that protects humanity from knowledge of the anomalous.

We Secure. We Contain. We Protect.

About the SCP Foundation

Established 1██9 | Operations: Global

Foundation History

The SCP Foundation was established in 1██9 by the original O5 Council following the consolidation of various occult and paranormal research organizations. The Foundation's creation was necessitated by a series of catastrophic anomalous events that threatened global stability and normalcy.

The Foundation currently operates [REDACTED] facilities across 7 continents, employing over [REDACTED] personnel. Our operations are overseen by the O5 Council, whose identities remain classified at Level 5 clearance.

Core Values

Secure

We secure anomalies that pose a threat to humanity. Through advanced containment methods and specialized facilities, we ensure these objects remain isolated from the public.

Contain

We contain the knowledge and existence of these anomalies. Information control and memetic countermeasures prevent widespread panic and societal collapse.

Protect

We protect humanity from threats beyond normal comprehension. Our personnel sacrifice their safety and sometimes their lives to maintain the illusion of a normal world.

Ethics and Oversight

The Foundation operates under strict ethical guidelines enforced by the Ethics Committee. While our mission sometimes requires difficult decisions, we are committed to minimizing harm and treating all subjects—human or otherwise—with the dignity circumstances allow.

All operations are subject to oversight by the O5 Council, consisting of 13 Level 5 personnel who hold absolute authority over Foundation policy and strategic direction. The Council operates above all other Foundation authority, including Site Directors and Department Heads.

Global Operations

The Foundation maintains operations across all seven continents, with primary facilities in North America, Europe, and Asia. Mobile Task Forces are deployed globally to respond to containment breaches and secure newly discovered anomalies. All operations report directly to O5 Command.
